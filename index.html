📖 Game Rules

  1. Classic Mode is the original 10-question game.
  2. Survival Mode contains up to 20 questions.
  3. Time Attack gives you 60 seconds.
  4. Daily Challenge can be completed once per day.
  5. Hard Mode uses only unique capital/country questions.
  6. Correct answers give Score, XP and Coins.
  7. Wrong answers reset Combo and reduce Lives.
  8. Chests can be earned or purchased.
  9. Chest rarities: Common, Rare, Epic, Legend, Mythic, Divine.
  10. Higher ranks provide XP and Coin bonuses.
