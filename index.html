Game Rules

Everything you need to know.

  1. Each game contains 10 questions.
  2. You start with 3 Lives.
  3. Correct answers give Score, XP and Coins.
  4. Correct answers increase your Combo.
  5. Wrong answers reset your Combo and normally cost one Life.
  6. Hint removes two incorrect answers and costs 25 Score.
  7. Coins can be spent in the Shop.
  8. XP increases your Level.
  9. Your Level determines your Rank.
  10. Difficulty changes the question types and Score reward.
  11. Achievements provide additional Coins and XP rewards.
