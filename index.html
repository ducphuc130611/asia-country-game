📖 Game Rules

  1. Classic Mode contains 10 questions.
  2. Classic Mode has a 12-second timer.
  3. Easy, Normal and Hard change question types.
  4. Hard Currency and Region questions use a unique capital clue.
  5. Survival continues until you lose all lives.
  6. Time Attack gives limited time to answer questions.
  7. Sudden Death ends after the first wrong answer.
  8. Endless Mode has no fixed question limit.
  9. Bosses become stronger according to their rarity.
  10. Correct boss answers deal damage to the boss.
  11. Stronger bosses contain more questions.
  12. Items can be used from the Inventory.
  13. Mega Hint removes three incorrect answers.
  14. Skins provide permanent bonuses.
  15. Chest rarity determines reward quality.
  16. Transcendent Chests are extremely rare.
  17. Level has no maximum limit.
