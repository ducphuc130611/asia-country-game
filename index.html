📖 Game Rules

  1. Classic Mode contains 10 questions.
  2. You start with 3 Lives.
  3. Correct answers give Score, XP and Coins.
  4. Combo increases your rewards.
  5. Hard Mode contains Currency and Region questions.
  6. Currency and Region questions use an additional capital clue to guarantee a unique answer.
  7. Boss Mode contains powerful bosses.
  8. Bosses reward Coins, XP, Chests and exclusive items.
  9. Skins provide permanent bonuses.
  10. Daily Challenge can be completed once per day.
