📖 Game Rules

  1. Each game contains 10 questions.
  2. You start with 3 lives.
  3. Correct answers give Score, XP and Coins.
  4. Combo increases your score reward.
  5. Losing all lives ends the game.
  6. Hint removes two incorrect answers.
  7. Extra Life gives one additional life.
  8. Double XP doubles one XP reward.
  9. Score Boost increases one score reward.
  10. Second Chance prevents one life loss.
  11. Lucky Answer saves one wrong answer.
  12. Coins can be spent in the Shop.
  13. XP increases your Level.
