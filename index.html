📖 Game Rules

  1. Each game contains 10 questions.
  2. You start with 3 lives.
  3. Correct answers give Score, XP and Coins.
  4. Consecutive correct answers increase your Combo.
  5. Easy uses capital questions.
  6. Normal uses capital and currency questions.
  7. Hard uses all question types.
  8. Hint removes two incorrect answers.
  9. Extra Life gives +1 life at the beginning of a game.
  10. Double XP doubles XP from one correct answer.
  11. Score Boost increases one score reward by 25%.
  12. Second Chance prevents one life loss.
  13. Lucky Answer can save one wrong answer.
  14. Coins can be spent in the Shop.
  15. XP increases your Level.
  16. Level determines your Rank.
